## Corrections made

**Girls' fixed wardrobe:** south-wall footprint is reserved, at the owner-confirmed 600 mm depth with sliding doors. Its width is represented by the nominal wall bay, with an approximate switch strip retained. Exact wardrobe height, finish and end positions remain illustrative; nothing new is placed in its bay. Existing wardrobe contents are not counted as spare capacity.

**Two retained metal cupboards:** moved to the reported available west wall. Combined run 1800 mm within a 2200 mm candidate segment. The low bed remains a 2050 × 950 mm proxy; full pull-out width is 1900 mm.

**Night access:** about 910 mm from wardrobe front to bed foot. With the lower mattress out, the gap to the west cupboard fronts is only 270 mm. It is not presented as a walking route. Use the foot of the mattresses to enter/leave. Full metal-cupboard door access requires storing the pull-out bed. This is a real use restriction, not a passed all-modes clearance. If full cupboard access while both beds are out is required, the two-cupboard reuse arrangement needs a different solution.

**Study equipment:** both stacks are freestanding at the owner's 2000 mm overall height, with no cupboard around or shelves above them. Each stack visibly contains two four-drawer modules, one multi-depth three-drawer module, and two cabinets on top. The stack bodies are simplified models; module proportions are distributed within the retained overall height rather than claiming an exact interlock/base reconstruction.

**Study 2200 mm run:** 900 mm existing cupboard + two 600 mm-wide stack planning envelopes + two 50 mm gaps = 2200 mm. Each stack reserves 450 mm depth; a 450 mm forward drawer-use allowance is shown in the checks. Exact segment endpoints, installed accessories and full drawer travel still need confirmation. Other study walls remain outside the design.

**Study-box contents:** the removed upper archive shelves are replaced by a new 800 W × 500 D × 2100 H mm living-room archive-cupboard proxy. No box contents disappear to make the study fit. This adds a tall item beside the living toy cupboard and changes the living-room storage character compared with iteration 01.
