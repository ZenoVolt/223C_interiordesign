## Girls' room — low pull-out bed, day and night

- No bunk. The proxy is a 2050 × 950 mm low bed with a second 950 mm mattress pulling out to the west, for a 1900 mm total sleeping footprint when open. Actual product, mattress heights, rails and operating envelope remain to be selected.
- Two existing full cupboards are reused on the **south wall**, rather than the previously discussed 2200 mm west-wall area. This preserves the west route beside the open bed.
- The south wall is nominally about 1867 mm clear in the model; the cupboard pair is 1800 mm. The remaining approximately 67 mm is tight and must cover real cupboard widths, jambs, skirting and gaps. Do not order or move on the strength of the render alone.
- The fully open bed leaves approximately **717 mm at its west side** in the model. There is approximately **1070 mm between cupboard fronts and the bed foot**. These are measured concept gaps, not guarantees of comfort or compliance.
- Daily toy contents are provisionally assigned to new living-room cupboard storage; the two reused cupboards retain their existing contents. No spare capacity is assumed inside them. Girls use the dining table, so no desk is added.
- The original stainless appearance is represented honestly. The warm-wood bed and textiles are proposed loose-furniture finishes, not a selected style or changes to paint/lighting.

## Study — only the 2200 mm hinge-side wall

The plan interprets the permitted wall as the east wall beside the existing study-door hinge. The run is placed north of the open leaf: **900 mm existing cupboard + 1300 mm new utility furniture = 2200 mm**. Its exact along-wall endpoints need owner/site confirmation; the rest of the study is hatched as outside this redesign.

The new utility proxy is 1300 W × 700 D × 2200 H mm, with two lower equipment bays for the Packout stacks and upper storage for the eleven study-box contents. This can become suitable loose furniture or modular units after real dimensions/products are checked; it is not a promised off-the-shelf product. Each Packout stack currently uses an explicitly assumed 600 W × 600 D × 1050 H mm envelope. If either actual stack exceeds it, this arrangement must be revised.

The proxy furniture starts about 227 mm beyond the modelled open door-leaf tip along the wall. The full modelled swing is clear in the footprint check. No desk or furniture is added to another study wall.

## Validation and visual limits

- [Fit checks](fit_checks.json): 27 checks cover wall/furniture footprint intersections, sampled full door sweeps, play-zone obstructions, retained-item counts, storage-run length and unchanged baseline bytes. Both options pass. Intentional equipment containment in utility furniture is checked separately.
- [Saved-file audit](saved_file_audit.json): reopens the actual Blender files and compares 246 architecture meshes against the saved shell. All compared vertex geometry is unchanged, and study storage measures 2200 mm along the wall in both saved models.
- The architectural shell is inherited, including its provisional window elevations, door dimensions and some beam drops. These checks establish internal consistency with that shell, not site accuracy.
- Images use consistent presentation light and approximated Barely There / white marble-look floor materials. Installed lights, tile format, sheen, curtains and exact colour appearance were not surveyed. No physical new lighting or fixed-finish work is proposed.
- Furniture models are intentionally simplified proxies. Recliner, bed, utility cupboard and all new pieces need actual dimensions and appropriate manufacturer installation/use requirements checked before selection.
- No product shortlist, current price estimate or purchase is included in this pass.
