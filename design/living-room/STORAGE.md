## What the box dimensions establish

[IKEA SAMLA 701.809.41](https://www.ikea.com/sg/en/p/samla-box-transparent-70180941/) measures 560 W × 390 D × 280 H mm, nominally 45 L. The nominal total is 945 L: 450 L for ten toy boxes and 495 L for eleven study boxes. These are container capacities, not measured quantities of contents or a cabinet-sizing formula.

If a later option retains some boxes intact, check cupboard **clear internal** dimensions, door opening and removal space. A 400 mm external cupboard depth does not guarantee room for a 390 mm-deep box behind doors/back panels. This does not constrain the current repacking approach: drawers, shelves and smaller trays can be designed around the contents instead.

The existing cupboards' combined outer volume is not spare or fully usable storage. Shelves, hinges, access and the actual mix of books/files/toys matter. Keep the existing full cupboard contents separate from the additional box contents in each allocation.
